# Capilora Professional — Backlink Strategy

Goal: earn authority links to `https://capilora-store.vercel.app` (and the custom domain once added) so the store ranks for product/commercial search terms in India.

## Who to target (realistic, relevant: Indian beauty/derm, salon trade)
- **Derma/beauty bloggers & Instagram-adjacent writers** who publish "best salon-grade hair care" / "best dandruff shampoo India" roundups. Their link footers / contributor bios are acquirable with a sample box.
- **Affiliate/review niches** (Beautylish-style, health blogs) — offer affiliate codes for these 8 products; links drive both direct sales and SEO.
- **Salon/trade directories** and supplier listicles targeted by our `intense-underarm-cream` and salon partnership angle.
- **Local news / PIJs** (partner journalism) quoting founder for "new Indian skin-care brand made in Bharuch/Gujarat" angles — cheap, gets a `.in` news domain link.

## Assets likely to earn links (build once, link-getting forever)
- **The 8 clean product packshots** (already generated) — offer downloadable high-res versions to bloggers.
- **Ad creative kit** (`/ads`) — unique freebie, bloggers use it and link back.
- **"Proven actives" ingredient wiki-style page** (Piroctone Olamine, Niacinamide, AA2G) — link-bait reference resource; add an internal `/ingredients` page.
- **Dandruff-cause-and-cure explainer** targeting "dandruff" queries — shareable, links product pages.

## Tactics (in priority order)
1. **Listicle outreach:** email 20–30 Indian beauty bloggers with a sample box + ready-to-publish copy. One paragraph, one link. Cold outreach converts best on free product.
2. **Salon/bulk partnerships:** every salon or bulk client gets a "brands we trust" footer link on their site in exchange — usually a ready-made link for our salon angle.
3. **Business directories (NAP):** Google Business Profile (free, essential), Justdial, IndiaMART supplier listing — quick authority, low effort.
4. **Digital PR one-off:** a short "made in India family brand" pitch to local/state business media. One good story can yield 3–5 news links.
5. **Guest posts / contributor articles** on Indian beauty publications that accept writers (e.g. Femina-style online sites, beauty blogs with contributor programs).

## What NOT to do
- No PBNs, no cheap directory spam, no bought footer links — Google ignores or penalizes them and the store is young.
- Don't overload anchor text — mix branded ("Capilora Professional"), bare URL, and a little exact-match ("dandruff control shampoo India").

## Execution notes
- This is playbook, not automation: it needs a human to run outreach (no accounts/tools wired up).
- Add Google Search Console verification (auto path: Vercel → Settings → domains already serves the site; manual verify at search.google.com/search-console using the HTML-tag method → Domain property records DNS if the custom domain is added).

## Suggested cadence
- Week 1: Google Business Profile + directories + GitHub/blog profile links.
- Week 2: 20 blogger outreach emails + 3 guest-post pitches.
- Month 2+: 1 salon partnership + 1 digital-PR story; re-post new product launch to update listicles.
